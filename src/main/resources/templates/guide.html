Frequently Asked Questions

Are my resume and chat history saved if I close the browser?

Yes. Everything is stored server-side in MySQL — resumes, personal info, and AI chat conversations. Log back in from any browser and it's all there.

Why did the AI chat stop responding?

The free AI API has a daily quota. When it's exhausted the app falls back to built-in suggestions. The quota resets at midnight UTC, or you can add credits at openrouter.ai for a higher limit.

Can I use the resume for job applications (ATS)?

Yes. Templates use real selectable text, standard section names, and no columns that break parsers — exactly what ATS software expects.

How do I add LinkedIn / GitHub / Portfolio links?

Open the 🔗 Social Links section, click + Add Link, choose the platform from the dropdown, and paste the URL. They appear neatly spaced in the header of every template.

Can I create multiple resumes?

Yes — create as many as you like from the dashboard and tailor each one to a different job. The Duplicate action makes tailored variants fast.

Ready to build your resume?

It takes about 10 minutes to go from blank page to downloadable PDF.
